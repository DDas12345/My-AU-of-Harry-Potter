The colossal double doors swung inward, revealing the breathtaking majesty of the Great Hall. Four excessively long house tables stretched across the expanse, packed with hundreds of students under the warm glow of thousands of floating candles drifting lazily in midair. High above, where a stone ceiling should have been, a vast, star-dusted velvet cosmos mirrored the exact night sky outside.

Walking alongside Ruhi, Hermione leaned closer and whispered in a knowledgeable tone, "It's not real, the ceiling. It's just bewitched to look like the night sky. I read about it in *Hogwarts: A History*."

Ruhi looked up, her sharp senses appreciating the fine weave of illusion spellwork, though her mind remained centered on her core purpose.

Professor McGonagall led the long line of first-years straight to the front of the hall, where a four-legged wooden stool sat holding a ancient, patched, and frayed wizard’s hat.

"Will you wait along here, please?" McGonagall instructed, bringing the procession to a halt. "Now, before we begin, Professor Dumbledore would like to say a few words."

At the high staff table, Albus Dumbledore rose gracefully to his feet, his long silver beard glittering under the candlelight.

"I have a few start-of-term notices I wish to announce," Dumbledore's voice reverberated clearly through the silent hall. "In the first years, please note that the Dark Forest is strictly forbidden to all students. Also, our caretaker, Mr. Filch..." He gestured toward a ragged, scowling old man standing near the side doors, clutching a dust-colored cat with glowing, red lamp-like eyes. "...has asked me to remind you that the third-floor corridor on the right-hand side is out of bounds to everyone who does not wish to die a most painful death. Thank you."

He resumed his seat. While many first-years shuddered at the sight of the caretaker's creepy pet, Mrs. Norris, Ruhi observed the red-eyed feline with a quiet, appreciative tilt of her head. Coming from a world of mystical creatures, she found the cat's intense, watchful aura quite beautiful and remarkably cute.

Professor McGonagall unrolled a long scroll of parchment. "When I call your name, you will come forth. I shall place the Sorting Hat on your head, and you will be sorted into your houses. Hermione Granger."

Hermione gasped softly, wringing her hands nervously. "Oh, no. Okay, relax," she muttered to herself as she stepped forward.

Ron nudged Harry and muttered under his breath, "Mental, that one, I'm telling you."

Harry nodded in silent agreement, but Ruhi turned to them and corrected softly, "She is just relaxing her mind, Ron. Preparation is a virtue."

Hermione sat upon the stool, and McGonagall lowered the ancient leather hat onto her head.

The hat twitched slightly. "Ah, right then, hmm, right. Okay... GRYFFINDOR!"

The table on the far left erupted into loud cheers as Hermione let out a breathless sigh, jumped off the stool with a bright smile, and hurried over to take her seat.

"Draco Malfoy," McGonagall called next.

Draco sauntered up proudly, his shoulders squared with absolute confidence. The tattered hat had barely brushed his slicked-back blonde hair before it freaked and bellowed its decision into the hall.

"SLYTHERIN!"

The green-and-silver table clapped and cheered, though notably less thunderously than Gryffindor had.

Ron leaned into Harry and Ruhi, whispering grimly, "There isn't a witch or wizard who went bad who wasn't in Slytherin."

"Susan Bones," McGonagall called.

A small girl with red pigtails stumbled forward nervously. As Susan sat down, Harry’s eyes drifted toward the staff table. Standing near the center was a pale wizard with greasy, shoulder-length black hair and dark, penetrating eyes—Professor Severus Snape. Snape was staring directly at Harry with a cold, unreadable, almost sinister look.

Suddenly, a searing, white-hot spike of agony shot through the lightning-bolt scar on Harry's forehead.

"Ahh!" Harry gasped out loud, slapping a hand over his forehead and wincing hard.

Ron turned instantly, deeply concerned. "Harry, what is it?"

"Nothing... Nothing, I'm fine," Harry panted, rubbing his forehead as the pain subsided into a dull throb.

At that exact moment, Snape’s dark eyes shifted away from Harry and landed squarely on Ruhi. Behind his stoic, guarded mask, Snape experienced a sudden, hidden wave of shock. He recognized the divine, distinct presence immediately—this was the true heiress of Nageshwari. He masked his expression instantly, but his dark gaze lingered on her with heavy significance.

"Let's see... I know! HUFFLEPUFF!" the Sorting Hat announced for Susan Bones, breaking the tension.

"Ronald Weasley," McGonagall announced.

Ron gulped heavily, his face turning as pale as his freckles allowed. He walked up to the stool as if marching toward an executioner and sat down. The moment the hat touched his messy red hair, it spoke up.

"Ha! Another Weasley. I know just what to do with you... GRYFFINDOR!"

Ron let out a massive sigh of relief, his shoulders slumping as the Gryffindor table cheered loudly, his older brothers Fred and George whistling wildly.

"Harry Potter," McGonagall called out.

An instant, dead silence fell over the massive hall. Whispers rippled like wildfire across all four house tables. *“Potter? Did she say Potter?” “The Harry Potter?”*

Before Harry could step forward, frozen by the hundreds of staring eyes, Ruhi placed a gentle, reassuring hand on his arm. "May Mahadev grant you strength, Harry. Good luck," she spoke softly.

Nearby, Draco Malfoy caught the exchange and glared at Harry with intense jealousy, biting his lip in dark frustration as Harry stepped past him.

Harry sat on the stool. McGonagall dropped the heavy leather hat over his eyes.

"Hmm," a soft, dry voice echoed inside Harry’s mind. "Difficult, very difficult. Plenty of courage, I see. Not a bad mind, either. There's talent, oh, yes. And a thirst to prove yourself. But where to put you?"

Harry gripped the edges of the stool, repeating over and over in his mind, *Not Slytherin, not Slytherin!*

"Not Slytherin, eh?" the Sorting Hat questioned softly inside his head. "Are you sure? You could be great, you know. It's all here, in your head. And Slytherin will help you on the way to greatness! There's no doubt about that. No?"

*Please, not Slytherin, anything but Slytherin,* Harry pleaded desperately in his thoughts.

"Well, if you're sure... Better be... GRYFFINDOR!"

An absolute explosion of cheers, stomping feet, and roar echoed through the Great Hall. Harry stood up, beaming with immense relief, and hurried toward the Gryffindor table. Fred and George Weasley stood on their bench, chanting loudly, "We got Potter! We got Potter!"

Once the noise died down, McGonagall looked down at her parchment, her voice carrying a profound, rhythmic weight as she called the final name.

"Ruhi."

A quiet hush washed over the hall as Ruhi stepped out from the remaining line of students. Her walk was effortless, gliding forward with an ancient, royal elegance that demanded instant respect. She took her seat on the wooden stool, sitting with her back straight and her hands folded neatly in her lap.

McGonagall gently lowered the Sorting Hat onto Ruhi’s head.

The moment the magical fabric touched her hair, the Sorting Hat gave a sharp, audible gasp that vibrated directly against her crown. The ancient enchantments within the leather recoiled in absolute wonder as it felt the immense, cosmic ocean of divine power resting inside this eleven-year-old girl. It recognized her immediately as the royal Heiress of Nageshwari.

Lowering its voice to an impossibly low, reverent whisper that echoed only within Ruhi's mind, the Sorting Hat spoke:

*"You are neither strictly male nor female in your cosmic balance... You are neither a creation of mortal stories nor an ordinary human... You are neither an angel, a demon, nor a God, but an ardent, unwavering devotee of Lord Shiva. You are the partial incarnation of Sheshnaag—the most powerful ancient serpent of India, who carries the entire weight of the universe upon his majestic hood. You are the future Queen of the Realm of Serpents..."*

Sitting on the stool, Ruhi held her breath, her heart beating steadily. She let out a soft, silent sigh of relief when she realized the Hat was speaking to her telepathically. Had it proclaimed her true cosmic nature out loud to the entire Great Hall, her undercover mission to investigate her parents' murders would have been instantly destroyed.

The Sorting Hat continued its whisper, its voice filled with profound warmth and deep respect for her righteous soul:

*"Your heart is pure gold, little Naagrani. You will never strike down the innocent, yet you possess the fiery resolve to utterly destroy all evil..."*

The Hat opened its tear in the brim and shouted its verdict aloud for the entire castle to hear:

"GRYFFINDOR!"

The Gryffindor table broke into ecstatic applause once again. Ruhi offered a polite, dignified nod to Professor McGonagall, who gave her a subtle, proud look. Ruhi stepped down from the stool and walked gracefully toward the Gryffindor table, taking her seat right alongside Harry, Ron, and Hermione, ready to begin her true search in the shadows of Hogwarts.
