The bright Scottish sun shone down on the manicured grass of the Hogwarts grounds as the Gryffindor and Slytherin first-years lined up in two neat rows. Resting on the ground beside each student was a smooth wooden broomstick.

Marching briskly between the lines came their flying instructor, Madam Hooch. She possessed short, spiky gray hair and striking, hawk-yellow eyes that scanned the row of nervous students with sharp intensity.

"Good afternoon, class," Madam Hooch called out firmly.

"Good afternoon, Madam Hooch," the students chorused back.

"Good afternoon, Amanda. Good afternoon," Hooch nodded to a few students, stepping to the center of the field. "Welcome to your first flying lesson. Well, what are you waiting for? Everyone, step up to the left side of your broomstick. Come on now, hurry up. Stick your right hand over the broom and say, 'Up!'"

"Up!" echoed across the grounds as twenty voices shouted at their brooms.

Harry held his right hand out over his broomstick. "Up!" he commanded clearly. Instantly, the broom handle snapped upward directly into his palm. Harry’s eyes widened in sheer amazement. "Wow!"

Standing right next to him, Ruhi aligned her posture, holding her hand out with calm, absolute authority. "Up!" she declared.

With a sharp *swish*, her broom handle rose fluidly into her waiting hand as if answering a royal command.

Hermione, whose broom had merely rolled over lazily in the grass, stared at both Harry and Ruhi with a mixture of disbelief and intense intellectual frustration.

A few spots down, Draco Malfoy smirked. "Up!" His broom leaped into his hand cleanly. He adjusted his collar, tossing a haughty, arrogant grin directly toward Ruhi as if trying to assert his superiority over her. Ruhi ignored him entirely, her eyes fixed forward.

"Up!" Ron barked, bending over his broom. "Up!"

"With feeling!" Madam Hooch barked back, pacing down the line.

Hermione glared down at her stubborn broomstick. "Up. Up. Up. Up."

Ruhi turned slightly, offering Hermione a warm, encouraging smile. "Relax your mind, Hermione. Speak to it with confidence, not demand."

Ron gritted his teeth, thrusting his hand forward with annoyance. "Up!!"

*THWACK!*

The broom handle shot straight up like a wooden missile and smacked Ron dead across the bridge of his nose!

"Oh!" Ron yelped, clutching his face while his eyes watered.

Harry and Ruhi both broke into a sudden burst of laughter. Ron glared at them through his fingers, rubbing his nose. "Shut up, Harry and Ruhi!" A second later, as the sting faded, Ron couldn't help but chuckle at his own misfortune.

Madam Hooch stepped back to the head of the class. "Now, once you've got hold of your broom, I want you to mount it. And grip it tight; you don't want to be sliding off the end."

The class swung their legs over the handles, straddling their brooms.

"When I blow my whistle, I want each of you to kick off from the ground, hard," Hooch instructed. "Keep your broom steady, hover for a moment, then lean forward slightly and touch back down. On my whistle. 3, 2..."

*FWEEEEEEET!*

Before Madam Hooch could count down, a sharp gasp cut through the air. Neville Longbottom’s broomstick suddenly gave a violent shudder, lifting him off the turf before anyone else had even moved. Neville’s feet dangled helplessly, his face turning pale with sheer terror.

"Oh..." Neville squeaked.

"Mr. Longbottom!" Hooch called out, stepping forward.

"Neville, what are you doing?" a Gryffindor girl gasped.

"We're not supposed to take off yet!" a boy shouted.

Neville began rising steadily higher into the air, his knuckles turning white as he gripped the handle for dear life. "M-M-M-Mr. Longb—!" Hooch stammered, holding up her hands.

"AHH!" Neville screamed as the broom suddenly surged upward like an unbroken wild horse.

"Mr. Longbottom!" Hooch yelled.

"Down! Down! Ahhhh!" Neville shrieked, floating twenty feet above their heads.

Harry took a step forward. "Neville!"

Seeing her classmate in mortal danger, Ruhi’s golden-brown eyes flashed. Her Ichhadhaari Naagin instincts urged her to act; she began to quietly channel her divine cosmic levitation, ready to wrap Neville in a safe, invisible barrier of energy to glide him down.

Madam Hooch’s sharp hawk-eyes caught the sudden, supernatural shift in the air around Ruhi. Hooch instantly held out a stern, stopping hand toward Ruhi, giving her a sharp, silent look that warned her strictly *not* to reveal her magical secrets in front of the entire class. Ruhi held her breath, suppressing her aura, and stepped back.

"Mr. Longbottom, exactly where do you think you're going?!" Hooch shouted up at the sky.

"Help!!!" Neville screamed as his broom rocketed straight toward the castle towers!

"Come back down this instant!" Hooch demanded, running along the grass.

"AHH!" Neville’s broom slammed violently against the stone battlements, scraping along the outer walls as he clung to the wood, screaming in absolute terror. The broom flipped end over end, zooming right back down toward the group of panicked students.

"Help!" Neville screamed.

Hooch whipped out her wand, stepping forward to catch him. "Mr. Longbottom—!"

"Look out!" Harry yelled as the broom swooped violently low.

Students scattered in every direction. Madam Hooch had to dive flat onto the grass to avoid being decapitated as Neville shot past, careening toward a courtyard statue of a medieval warrior holding a sharp spear.

Neville’s black robes caught right on the spear tip! The sudden jerk ripped him from the broom, leaving him dangling upside down in mid-air thirty feet above the stone courtyard.

"Oh! Ah, help!" Neville sobbed, swinging wildly.

*RIP!*

The fabric tore through the spearhead. Neville plummeted straight down! Fortunately, his robe snagged briefly on a lower iron wall torch, slowing his fall for a fraction of a second before the cloth gave way entirely.

*CRASH!*

Neville hit the hard turf with a sickening thud, letting out a pitiful groan. "Ow!"

"Everyone out of the way!" Madam Hooch shouted, scrambling to her feet and pushing through the crowd. She knelt beside the weeping boy, carefully inspecting his arm.

Hermione rushed forward. "Is he alright?"

"Ow, ow, ow, ow!" Neville sobbed, clutching his wrist.

"Oh, oh, oh, oh dear. It's a broken wrist," Hooch tutted softly, gently pulling him up. "Tch, tch, tch. Poor boy. Come on now, up you get."

As Hooch helped Neville to his feet, Draco Malfoy reached down and snatched a small glass sphere wrapped in gold from the grass—Neville’s Remembrall. He slipped it behind his back with a nasty grin.

Hooch turned to face the class, pointing a stern finger. "Everyone's to keep their feet firmly on the ground while I take Mr. Longbottom to the hospital wing. Understand? If I see a single broom in the air, the one riding it will find themselves out of Hogwarts before they can say, *Quidditch*."

She turned and guided the limping, tearful Neville away toward the castle doors.

The moment Hooch was out of earshot, Draco stepped to the center of the lawn, tossing the glass sphere into the air and catching it, letting out a sharp snicker. "Did you see his face? Maybe if the fat lump had squeezed this, he'd have remembered to fall on his fat arse!"

Crabbe and Goyle burst into boisterous laughter. Harry and Ruhi stepped out of the group together, their faces hardened with absolute anger.

"Give it here, Malfoy," Harry demanded coldly.

Draco’s smirk widened. "No. I think I'll leave it somewhere for Longbottom to find." He mounted his broom with effortless ease, kicked off the ground, and began circling high above the group like a vulture. "How about on the roof?" he taunted, hovering twenty feet in the air. "What's the matter, Potter? Bit beyond your reach?"

Draco then turned his gaze straight down at Ruhi, his eyes glittering with arrogance and dark obsession. He puffed out his chest, calling out loudly so the whole courtyard could hear:

"How about a challenge, Potter? If I win this duel... Ruhi belongs to ME!"

A collective gasp of shock rippled through the Gryffindors and Slytherins alike. Ron’s jaw dropped in disgust, while Hermione’s eyes widened in horror at Malfoy’s outrageous, arrogant claim.

Ruhi’s golden-brown eyes darkened with lethal, royal fury. The sheer audacity of this boy treating a future Naagrani like a trophy to be won lit a fiery inferno in her chest.

Harry’s green eyes flashed with protective rage. Without a single word of hesitation, Harry grabbed his broom. Ruhi swung her leg over her own handle at the exact same moment.

Hermione rushed forward, grabbing at their sleeves. "Harry, Ruhi, no way! You heard what Madam Hooch said! Besides, you don't even know how to fly—!"

Ignoring her entirely, Harry and Ruhi kicked off the turf together, shooting into the sky like twin arrows.

Hermione stomped her foot in exasperation. "What an idiot!"

Up in the air, the cool autumn wind whipped through Harry’s black hair and Ruhi’s long locks. Harry pulled up right across from Draco, his face set in stone.

"Give it here, Malfoy," Harry snarled, "or I'll knock you off your broom!"

"Is that so?" Draco smirked, spinning his broom around in a graceful, arrogant 360-degree loop. "Have it your way, then!"

With all his strength, Draco hurled the Remembrall high into the air, launching it like a baseball straight toward the grand stone tower of the castle!

Draco laughed triumphantly, expecting the ball to smash against the glass. But he completely underestimated the duo facing him.

Harry kicked his heels, zooming forward at breakneck speed, leaning flat against the handle as he pursued the sphere in a steep dive.

Meanwhile, Ruhi didn't just fly—she *commanded* the sky. Utilizing her supreme, fluid spatial awareness and the innate serpentine agility of an Ichhadhaari Naagin, Ruhi executed a breathtaking, high-speed tactical interception. She rocketed upward along the castle wall, taking a higher, sharper angle.

As Harry sped toward the tower window, Ruhi swooped down in a perfect, synchronized flank, using the wind resistance off her broom to cut off the air turbulence and create a slipstream for Harry. Her incredible maneuver stabilized Harry’s trajectory, giving him the exact angle and split-second boost he needed.

Just inches before the glass window—where Professor McGonagall was sitting at her desk working—Harry reached out his hand and caught the Remembrall cleanly out of the air!

Inside the office, McGonagall gasped, rising from her chair in absolute shock as she watched through the pane: Harry pulling off a miraculous dive, and Ruhi executing a flawless, professional-grade tactical Chaser flank to guide him through the turn!

Harry and Ruhi pulled up together, leveling their brooms, and swooped down toward the ground in a triumphant, breathtaking arc.

They landed smoothly on the lawn side-by-side. The Gryffindor students erupted into wild, ecstatic cheering, rushing forward to surround them.

"Nice going, Harry! Ruhi!" a boy cheered.

"Oh, that was wicked, Harry!" another boy yelled. "And Ruhi, you have slayed it! That flying maneuver was insane!"

Ron slapped Harry on the back, while Parvati and the Gryffindor girls gathered around Ruhi in awe of her unbelievable aerial control.

Across the lawn, Draco landed his broom, his face pale with humiliation and sheer frustration. Not only had he lost the Remembrall, but seeing Ruhi fly with such breathtaking, untouchable brilliance alongside Harry drove him mad with jealousy.

Suddenly, the crowd fell dead silent.

Professor McGonagall was striding across the lawn, her face unreadable and her emerald robes swishing violently against the grass.

"Harry Potter! Ruhi!" McGonagall called out sternly.

Harry and Ruhi exchanged a nervous glance, stepping out of the crowd.

"Follow me," McGonagall ordered sharply.

Sullenly, expecting the absolute worst, Harry and Ruhi carried their brooms and followed behind McGonagall as she led them back into the castle corridors. Draco and Crabbe snickered in delight from the sidelines, though Draco’s jaw remained tight with bitter envy.

McGonagall led them down the third-floor corridor, bringing them to a halt right outside Professor Quirrell’s Defense Against the Dark Arts classroom. Through the open door, Quirrell could be seen stammering through a lecture while holding a large green reptile.

"...an iguana s-such as this is..." Quirrell was saying.

McGonagall turned to the two first-years. "You both wait here."

She stepped into the doorway, raising her voice. "Professor Quirrell, excuse me, excuse me. Could I borrow Wood for a moment, please?"

Quirrell blinked nervously. "Oh, y-yes, of course." He turned back to the class as a tall, broad-shouldered fifth-year boy got up from his desk. "...And the vampire b-bat..."

As Oliver Wood exited the classroom, McGonagall gestured toward Harry and Ruhi, a brilliant, rare spark of pride shining in her stern eyes.

"Potter and Ruhi, this is Oliver Wood," McGonagall announced, her voice full of triumph. "Wood, I’ve found you a Seeker... and a Chaser!"
