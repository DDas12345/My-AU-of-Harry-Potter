The bright autumn sun illuminated the crowded stone courtyard as streams of students moved between their afternoon classes. Leaves of amber and rust drifted gently across the cobblestones, swept along by a crisp Scottish breeze.

Walking in a small group near the central fountain were Neville, Harry, Ruhi, Ron, and Seamus. While Neville was quietly reviewing his Herbology notes and Seamus was still picking tiny flecks of soot from his robes, Ron was marching ahead, his face flushed with lingering embarrassment from Charms class.

Unable to contain his bruised pride any longer, Ron tossed his head back, puffed out his chest, and began mimicking Hermione's precise, intellectual tone in an exaggerated, shrill voice.

"It's Levi-*ooo*-sa, not Leviosa*aaar*!" Ron sneered, before dropping back into his normal voice with a harsh, scoffing laugh. "She's a nightmare, honestly! No wonder she hasn't got any friends!"

Neville looked down uncomfortably, pulling his satchel strap tighter, while Seamus gave a weak, awkward chuckle.

Ruhi’s golden-brown eyes snapped toward Ron, her brow furrowing with sudden, fierce indignation. She opened her mouth to instantly scold him for his cruelty, but before the words could pass her lips, a sudden movement in the crowd caught Harry’s eye.

Hermione Granger burst through the archway, clutching her thick leather-bound textbooks tightly against her chest. Tears were streaming down her reddened cheeks, and her head was lowered as she sniffled quietly, trying desperately to hide her heartbreak from the passing students.

She had heard every single word.

Harry turned around, his face falling as he watched her. "I think she heard you."

Without looking back, Hermione pushed her way past a group of upperclassmen and disappeared down the sunlit corridor toward the girls' dormitories, her shoulders shaking with muffled sobs.

The atmosphere in the courtyard instantly turned icy cold.

Ruhi stopped dead in her tracks. Her posture straightened into a rigid, royal stance, and her golden-brown eyes burned with a piercing, lethal intensity that made even the passing breeze feel suddenly chilly.

Ron blinked, shuffling his feet awkwardly as he realized the weight of what had just happened. "Well... I mean... she was just being—"

"Silence, Ronald!" Ruhi’s voice cut through the courtyard air like a honed steel blade. It wasn't shouted, but it possessed such supreme, ancient authority that Neville and Seamus froze instantly, while Ron recoiled half a step.

Ruhi stepped directly into Ron's path, looking down at him with the unyielding majesty of a Naaglok princess who would never tolerate cruelty toward the innocent.

"Have you no shame?" Ruhi demanded, her tone dripping with quiet, righteous fury. "Hermione was born into a Muggle world, stepping into a magical reality that is completely foreign to her. Answering every question, mastering every book, and guiding those who make mistakes is not showing off—it is her way of communicating with this world! It is how she reaches out, connects with her teachers and peers, and proves to herself that she belongs here!"

Ron swallowed hard, his neck flushing red. "Ruhi, come on, she was—"

"She was *correcting* you because your pronunciation was entirely wrong!" Ruhi interjected, her eyes flashing with a faint, dangerous golden ember. "And why did she demonstrate the spell in the first place, Ron? Because *you* provoked her! *You* challenged her! You sneered and commanded her, *'You do it, then, if you're so clever!'* And when she simply reached out to help you learn, instead of reflecting on your own mistake, you let your fragile ego bleed into malicious insults!"

Ron lowered his gaze to the cobblestones, unable to meet Ruhi's fiery stare. "I... I didn't mean to make her cry..."

"Words spoken out of bruised pride cut deeper than iron blades, Ronald," Ruhi stated coldly, her voice softening slightly into a profound, moral lesson. "She was trying to build a bridge to connect with you, yet you shattered her spirit simply because your ego could not bear to be corrected. True strength lies in accepting guidance, not in tearing down those who are just trying to find their place."

Harry stepped up beside Ruhi, looking at Ron with a heavy, disappointed expression. "She's right, Ron. Hermione was only trying to connect and help."

Ron rubbed the back of his neck sheepishly, looking genuinely remorseful now as he stared down the empty corridor where Hermione had disappeared. "I... I'll apologize to her... at the feast tonight."

Ruhi let out a slow, steady breath, settling her ancient aura back into her serene human form. She looked toward the dark stone archway, her heart aching for Hermione's tears.

"Words are easily spoken, Ron, but actions rebuild broken trust," Ruhi spoke softly, offering a final, firm reminder. "You must make amends."

Turning to Harry, Ruhi's eyes met his green gaze, exchanging a quiet, unspoken understanding. In that sunlit courtyard, Ruhi had shown once again that she was not just a powerful aerial warrior and an Ichhadhaari Naagin heiress, but the moral heart and fiercely protective anchor of their group.
