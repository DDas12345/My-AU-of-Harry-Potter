The warm afternoon sun streamed through the high archways of the crowded Hogwarts corridors, casting golden light across the bustling stone hallways. Students were streaming between classes, whispering excitedly among themselves. Floating overhead, Sir Nicholas and a tall, shimmering lady ghost glided effortlessly through the air, their silver forms flickering in the sunlight.

"Have you heard?" Sir Nicholas announced proudly to the lady ghost, his voice echoing over the quiet chatter below. "Harry Potter's the new Gryffindor Seeker, and Ruhi is the Chaser! I always knew he'd do well, but Ruhi... she is unbelievable!"

Directly below them, Harry, Ruhi, and Ron were walking together through the stone archway. Ron was practically bouncing on his heels, his eyes wide with absolute disbelief.

"Seeker and Chaser?" Ron gasped, looking between his two friends. "But first-years *never* make their house teams! You both must be the youngest Quidditch players in—"

"—a century, according to McGonagall!" Harry and Ruhi chimed in together.

The exact same sentence left their lips at the exact same moment, spoken with the exact same rhythm. They stopped walking, turned to look at each other, and broke into a quiet, synchronized laugh, their eyes shining with shared amusement.

Before Ron could recover from their uncanny teamwork, Fred and George Weasley fell into step right beside them, slinging their arms over Ron's and Harry's shoulders.

"Hey, well done, Harry!" Fred clapped Harry on the back. "Wood's just told us! And brilliant flying out there, Ruhi!"

Ron looked up at his older brothers. "Fred and George are on the team, too. Beaters."

"Our job is to make sure that you both don't get bloodied up too bad," George explained with a wide, dramatic grin. "Can't make any promises, of course. Rough game, Quidditch."

Fred nodded solemnly, leaning in close with a wicked smirk. "Brutal. But no one's died in years. Someone will vanish occasionally..."

The twins suddenly broke away, walking backward down the sunlit corridor with synchronized, teasing waves.

"...but they'll turn up in a month or two!" George called out before the pair rounded the corner, their laughter echoing down the hallway.

Ron shook his head, turning back to Harry and Ruhi as they walked into the open courtyard. "Oh, go on, Harry and Ruhi. Quidditch is great. Best game there is! And you both will be great, too!"

Sitting at a nearby stone bench, Hermione Granger had her heavy textbook unrolled. Hearing the familiar panic in their voices, she snapped her book shut, jumped up from her work, and trotted across the courtyard to join them.

Harry rubbed the back of his neck, his green eyes dark with anxiety. "But I've never even played Quidditch. What if I make a fool of myself?"

Ruhi tilted her head, her voice calm and thoughtful as she shared his apprehension. "I have never played these Western aerial sports either, Harry. In India, our traditions are quite different. I only knew the word *Quidditch* as a flying sport from books, and until an hour ago, I had barely learned how to hold a broom handle, let alone fly."

Hermione offered Ruhi a warm, deeply reassuring smile, stepping beside her. "Once you take to the air and let your natural reflexes guide you, Ruhi, your instincts will take over completely. You already possess the grace and speed for it."

She then turned to Harry, her tone firm and encouraging. "And as for you, Harry... you won't make a fool of yourself. It's in your blood."

Harry and Ruhi exchanged a puzzled glance. Hermione gave a mysterious smirk and motioned for them to follow her.

---

A short while later, Hermione led the trio into a quiet display hall lined with ornate glass trophy cases. Shining golden cups, silver shields, and antique plaques rested on red velvet shelves, commemorating decades of Hogwarts champions.

Hermione walked straight to a massive wooden display frame, tapping her finger against the polished glass.

Harry and Ron leaned in close. Inside the case sat a pristine brass plaque listing the past legendary Gryffindor Quidditch players. Right near the top, engraved in bold lettering, was the name: **JAMES POTTER - SEEKER**.

Ron gasped, his jaw dropping open. "Whoa. Harry, you never told me your father was a Seeker, too!"

Harry stared at the glowing letters, his chest swelling with profound awe. "I didn't know..."

Standing right next to Harry, Ruhi’s golden-brown eyes drifted along the brass plaque, reading the names recorded over the decades. Suddenly, her breath hitched. Her heart skipped a violent beat.

Engraved clearly in golden script on the historic roster—listed as one of Gryffindor's most legendary Chasers—was the name: **NAGESHWARI**.

Ruhi’s hand trembled slightly beneath her dark sleeve. A wave of bittersweet, overwhelming emotion crashed over her. Her late mother, the divine Ichhadhaari Naagin who had come to Hogwarts decades ago, had flown across those very grounds as a Gryffindor Chaser!

Her mother’s blood—the blood of a fearless aerial warrior—was flowing directly through Ruhi’s veins. Flying wasn't just a sport; it was a sacred legacy connecting her to her mother’s footsteps.

Though her soul yearned to weep tears of profound grief and pride, Ruhi’s ancient warrior discipline held firm. She locked her intense emotions deep within her chest, maintaining her serene, regal composure. She could not reveal her mother's true identity to the castle yet.

Ruhi turned her head and looked at Harry, a soft, radiant smile touching her lips. In that silent moment, as they stood before the records of their lost parents, an unbroken, sacred thread connected their destinies even tighter. Both of them were carrying the torch of their lineage into the sky.
